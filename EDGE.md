# Where Pulse's edge can grow (research synthesis, Oct 2026)

Niche: US startups under 200 employees that expect to scale. Six research tracks: buyer pains, competitor review gaps, young-employee demand, scaling transitions, 2026 policy, benefits as recruiting/retention. Vendor-sourced numbers are flagged.

## Ranked features
| # | Feature | Evidence it rests on | Hard for incumbents to copy? |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Renewal vs CHOICE switch analyzer + PEO exit planner + ZIP-level "ICHRA fit" report | Small-group insurers asked a median 14% for 2027 (KFF via Insurance Business); Reddit renewals of 20-63%; PEO teaser rates and non-renewals; mid-year exits reset deductibles; 2/3 of small ICHRA adopters had no prior coverage, ~1/3 left small group (HRA Council via Take Command) | Hard for PEOs and Justworks (cannibalizes their pools); medium for Thatch |
| 2 | Premium payment ledger: carrier-direct pay, per-person status, grace-period alerts, ID-card SLA, payroll deduction reconciliation every run | Take Command autopay failures and BBB F; Venteur "failed to make payments"; Thatch ID-card and Insperity deduction delays; Gusto wrong-plan errors | Medium (it is operations, not code) |
| 3 | Scale engine: ALE forecast, per-person affordability (9.96% 2026, 10.22% 2027), class designer with min class sizes and whole-state exception (group at HQ + ICHRA remote), 1095-C | ALE set by prior-year average; 4980H penalties $3,340/$5,010 (2026); class rules and 4980D $100/day exposure | Medium; strongest expression of "grows with you" |
| 4 | CHOICE + bronze + HSA + DPC bundle; rewards paid into the HSA | OBBBA: bronze/catastrophic HSA-eligible 2026, DPC up to $150/$300 a month, permanent telehealth safe harbor; 2026 benchmark premiums +26%, 2027 median +15% | Medium; needs counsel on premium-only design |
| 5 | Retention ROI ledger + retention-based fee-at-risk guarantee | Gusto: health insurance ~25% fewer first-year quits; replacement cost ~80% of salary for technical roles (Gallup); 68% of employers use performance-based digital-health contracts (PHTI) but none found for under 200 | Hard: Thatch sells admin, not outcomes; small groups lack claims data so claims-based guarantees don't work |
| 6 | Burnout signal v2: short survey + sleep + workload (not HRV alone), one-tap mental-health day or therapy, manager nudge | Burned-out employees 2.6x as likely to job-hunt (Gallup); 74% of Gen Z needed stress time off, 43% took it (Deloitte); Mayo review: no physiological marker reliably tracks burnout | Medium; trust and privacy design is the moat |
| 7 | Offer-letter benefits calculator + living total-rewards statement | 86% say choosing their own insurer matters in an offer, 87% would consider a reimbursement (Justworks/Harris 2026); "$280 tax allowance, no idea what that means" | Easy to copy, but cheap and high-leverage |
| 8 | Claims and denial helper: plain-English EOBs, drafted appeals | Of those denied, ~half didn't appeal, mostly not knowing how (Commonwealth Fund 2026) | Medium |
| 9 | GLP-1 and fertility pathways in the wallet (DTC GLP-1 ~$350/mo) with a participatory program | Large-employer GLP-1 coverage 72% to 60% (BGH); 27% steer to DTC; strong switch-jobs signals (vendor surveys) | Medium |
| 10 | Privacy by architecture: state-aware consent (WA MHMDA, NV, CT, BIPA), "what your employer sees", audited aggregates | Only 7% would share health data with a tech company (Rock Health); MHMDA private right of action | Medium; trust compounds |
| 11 | Fundraise/M&A data room: 5500 history, SPD/POP, 1095-C, class records | ACA penalties inherited; 5500 non-filing up to $300/day | Easy, but sticky |

Later or riskier: level-funded readiness score (HIPAA, stop-loss lasers), multi-state reporting (MA HIRD, NJ, CA), EOR handoff, CHOICE tax-credit tracker (not law as of Oct 2026).

## Change in the current demo
- Reward design: participatory and activity-based, paid outside the ICHRA (or into an HSA), alternatives automatic. Never vary the allowance by wearable results.
- Burnout: present as an early check-in prompt, add a survey input, no diagnostic language.
- Landing line "costs you less than health insurance today": replace with budget-predictability language; 2026-27 individual-market increases make the cheaper claim fragile.
- Market as "CHOICE (ICHRA)".

## Open questions for counsel
Same-terms rules for wellness rewards next to an ICHRA; premium-only ICHRA design that keeps HSA eligibility; HRV/heart rate as biometric data under BIPA-style laws.
